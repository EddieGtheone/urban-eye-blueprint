# Urban Eye Project Master Delta — Business Modernization Blueprint

**Decision date:** July 16, 2026

This delta supersedes earlier master-document language that identifies the $50 Website Clarity Check as Urban Eye’s primary public entry service.

## Replace the primary lead-generation model with

### The Urban Eye Business Modernization Blueprint

A free interactive planning tool that helps a business identify and sequence the next modernization priority across:

1. Website Redesign
2. PIM & eCommerce Optimization
3. Sales & Marketing Systems
4. Technology & AI Implementation

### Public promise

**See what your business should modernize next.**

### Deliverable

A personalized modernization profile, three priorities, one quick win, a 30/60/90-day roadmap, a recommended Urban Eye service path, and a downloadable PDF.

### Primary CTA

**Build Your Blueprint**

## Positioning changes

Retire as primary language:

- Website Clarity Check
- Start for $50
- Start with clarity
- Entry diagnosis
- Audit-first framing

Use instead:

- Business modernization
- Personalized planning
- Prioritized implementation
- Build in sequence
- Website, commerce, marketing, and operations

## Architecture addition

```text
blueprint.urbaneyebybrooks.com
└── Vercel
    └── Urban Eye Business Modernization Blueprint
```

The application writes qualified submissions into Supabase through a server-only route. Urban Eye Admin will later review and convert those submissions into company, contact, lead, quote, proposal, client, and project records.

## Commercial role

The Blueprint is free lead generation and qualification. It does not replace paid implementation work or automatically promise a consultation. Qualified submissions may move into a Blueprint Review, scoped discovery, quote, or nurture sequence.
