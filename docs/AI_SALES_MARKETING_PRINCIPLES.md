# Urban Eye AI Sales & Marketing Principles

Knowledge base that shapes how the Blueprint's AI report (and its deterministic
fallback) is written. The operative directives are encoded in
`lib/marketing-principles.ts` (`SALES_MARKETING_PRINCIPLES`) and injected into the
report generator's prompt in `lib/ai-report.ts`. This document is the human-readable
rationale behind them.

## Provenance

Distilled from an internal analysis of a modern marketing strategy talk
("Marketing Strategy For Beginners"), reviewed and adapted to Urban Eye's
July 2026 governing position. The talk's surface framing (five budget levels)
is not what we adopt; the durable ideas below are.

## The core idea we adopt

> Modern marketing advantage comes from turning real customer understanding into
> many informed creative experiments, connecting attention to commercial behavior,
> and organizing so useful evidence can change decisions quickly.

For Urban Eye, the Blueprint report is one expression of that: it should read like
a knowledgeable partner who understands the owner's problem, shows the opportunity
vividly, and makes starting with Urban Eye feel like the obvious next step.

## Voice directives (applied to every report)

- **Empathy first.** Start from the problem the owner already feels, in their own
  words, then name the change they want. Relevance beats cleverness.
- **One reader, one problem, one idea, one action.** Keep them focused on the
  single most valuable place to start — never a menu of everything.
- **Sell momentum, not a one-time fix.** Frame the work as a loop that compounds;
  the businesses that start now build a lead every week.
- **Make the stakes vivid (real FOMO).** Show what standing still quietly costs and
  what 90 focused days unlock. Urgency comes from real stakes, never invented numbers.
- **Speak in outcomes, not systems.** Translate anything technical into customers,
  reputation, time, and money. No jargon.
- **Lead to Urban Eye.** Make partnering feel like the obvious, exciting next step,
  grounded in what Urban Eye actually builds.
- **Proof over hype.** Anchor excitement in what's genuinely possible and in Urban
  Eye's real capability — warm and confident, never salesy or hollow.

## Non-negotiable guardrails (kept from the Blueprint Copy Contract)

Excitement never comes at the cost of trust:

- The deterministic scoring — pillar, urgency, scores, recommended service — stays
  authoritative. The AI elaborates it; it never re-decides it.
- Use only the questionnaire answers and the public homepage snapshot. Never invent
  customers, revenue, staff, products, technology, results, or website findings — and
  never fabricate numbers, savings, percentages, or performance claims.
- No prices; no legal, financial, security, accessibility, or compliance guarantees.
- Website observations trace to the snapshot, and note that only the public homepage
  was reviewed.

## The Urban Eye framing

Public brand stays broad — Urban Eye helps businesses modernize how they look, sell,
market, and operate — across four pillars: Website Redesigns, PIM & eCommerce
Optimization, Sales & Marketing Systems, and Technology & AI Implementations. But
each report speaks to one reader and one problem, and every path leads to one action:
**Start the Blueprint**, then a conversation with Urban Eye.
